    ABOUT BOCHABELO

    Family-run, kitchen-first, built for Gauteng events.

    Bochabelo Catering & Hiring Services started as a small home-kitchen catering outfit and grew into a full events partner — because clients kept asking us to also sort the tent, the tables and the chairs. Today we run both sides from one team, so your food and your furniture arrive from the same place, on the same schedule.

    • One point of contactYour caterer and your hire coordinator are the same phone call.
