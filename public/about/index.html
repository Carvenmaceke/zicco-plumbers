About Zicco Plumbers

A registered, licensed plumbing company based in Braamfontein, Johannesburg.

Request a quote WhatsApp us Call +27 81 440 5778

Who we are

Zicco Plumbers is a plumbing company based at 33 Jorissen Street, Geldenhuys Building, Braamfontein, Johannesburg, Gauteng. We install plumbing on new construction sites and in high-rise buildings, and fit hot water heat pumps, geysers, water booster pumps and metered showers. We also clear blocked drains, carry out water jetting and drain camera inspections, find leaks, install subsoil drainage and handle plumbing repairs and maintenance.

When you call or WhatsApp +27 81 440 5778, ask for Ntsikelelo Mpayipeli.

What you can expect

  • A PIRB-registered company (no. 72110)
  • A licensed plumber (licence 8610/19) who can sign plumbing COCs
  • Experience on construction sites and in high-rise buildings
  • A quote before work starts

Who we work for

Homeowners and tenants, contractors and developers, building owners and body corporates, and student accommodation providers across Johannesburg, including Sandton, Randburg, Fourways, Midrand, the Johannesburg CBD and Braamfontein.
