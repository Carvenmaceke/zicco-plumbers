Below-ground drainage in Johannesburg

Below-ground drainage carries everything from the building to the municipal sewer. We lay new underground drain lines, and replace and repair pipes that are broken, sagging or full of roots.

What we do

  • New underground drain and sewer lines for new buildings and extensions
  • Drainage laid in and under slabs before the concrete is poured
  • Replacing broken, collapsed or root-damaged drain pipes
  • Manholes, inspection eyes and rodding points
  • Gullies and yard drains
  • Connections to the municipal sewer

Finding the problem first

For drains that keep blocking, smell or cause sinking paving, a drain camera inspection shows exactly where the damage is, so we only dig where it's needed. Blockages can often be cleared with high-pressure water jetting before any digging.

Licensed for below-ground drainage

Our plumber's PIRB licence covers below-ground drainage, so new and repaired drain lines can be certified with a plumbing COC.

Areas we cover

We're based in Braamfontein and work across Johannesburg, including Braamfontein, Johannesburg CBD, Sandton, Randburg, Fourways, Midrand.

Questions about below-ground drainage

How do I know if my underground drain is broken?

Common signs are drains that keep blocking, sewer smells outside, damp or sinking paving near a drain line, and very green patches in the garden. A camera inspection confirms it.

Do you lay drainage on new building sites?

Yes. We lay below-ground drainage on construction sites, including in-slab drainage before the pour.
