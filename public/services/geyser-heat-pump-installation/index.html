Geyser repairs and replacement

  • Burst or leaking geysers replaced
  • No hot water: element and thermostat faults
  • Leaking pressure control valves, safety valves and vacuum breakers
  • Drip trays and overflow pipes fitted correctly
  • New geyser installations in new builds and renovations

If your geyser has burst, switch off the geyser at the electrical board and close the water supply to it, then call or WhatsApp us.

Hot water heat pumps

A heat pump heats water by taking heat from the surrounding air, much like an air conditioner in reverse. It typically uses around a third of the electricity of a standard geyser element to heat the same amount of water. We install heat pump systems and connect them to your existing or new geyser.

Heat pumps suit homes with high hot water use, blocks of flats, guest houses and student accommodation.

COC for geyser work

Hot water installations must be done to standard and certified. Our licensed plumber (licence 8610/19) can issue a plumbing Certificate of Compliance, which insurers usually ask for after a geyser replacement.

Questions about geysers & heat pumps

Do you repair geysers or only install them?

Both. We repair geyser faults such as elements, thermostats and valves, and replace geysers that have burst or reached the end of their life.

Is a heat pump worth it?

For households and buildings that use a lot of hot water, a heat pump uses much less electricity than a geyser element, so the running cost is lower. Ask us for a quote and we can talk through whether it suits your property.

Will I get a COC for my new geyser?

Yes. Our licensed plumber can issue a Certificate of Compliance for the installation.
