High-pressure water jetting in Johannesburg

Water jetting cleans the inside of drain and sewer pipes with a high-pressure stream of water, cutting through grease, silt and roots that rods and plungers leave behind.

Request a quote WhatsApp us Call +27 81 440 5778

What water jetting clears

  • Grease and fat build-up in kitchen and restaurant drains
  • Silt, sand and debris in outside drains and stormwater lines
  • Tree roots growing into drain joints
  • Scale and sludge in older pipes
  • Long sewer runs in complexes and buildings

Why jetting works better

Rods push a hole through a blockage. Jetting scours the whole inside of the pipe, so the drain flows at full capacity and stays clear for longer. It uses only water, so there are no harsh chemicals in your drains.

Jetting with a camera inspection

On drains that block repeatedly, we can follow jetting with a drain camera inspection to check the pipe is clean and undamaged.

Questions about high-pressure water jetting

Will water jetting damage my pipes?

Jetting is safe for drain pipes in sound condition. Where a pipe is badly cracked or broken, a camera inspection first shows whether it needs repair.

How often should drains be jetted?

It depends on how the drain is used. Restaurant kitchens and large buildings benefit from regular jetting; homes usually only need it when there's a problem.
