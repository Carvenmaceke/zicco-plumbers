Plumbing installations for new buildings & high-rise

Zicco Plumbers installs plumbing on new construction sites and in high-rise buildings across Johannesburg, working with contractors and developers from the slab to handover.

Request a quote WhatsApp us Call +27 81 440 5778

What we install

  • Below-slab and in-slab drainage, laid before the pour
  • Soil and waste stacks and branch drainage on every floor
  • Cold and hot water supply pipework and valves
  • Geysers, heat pumps and hot water systems
  • Water booster pumps for upper floors
  • Sanitaryware, wall-hung toilets and concealed cisterns
  • Metered showers for student accommodation
  • Subsoil drainage around foundations and basements

Working with contractors and developers

We work from your drawings and the building programme, coordinate with the other trades on site, and make sure the plumbing is ready to test and inspect at each stage. As a PIRB-registered company (no. 72110) with a licensed plumber (licence 8610/19), we can issue the plumbing Certificate of Compliance at completion.

Areas we cover

We're based in Braamfontein and work across Johannesburg, including Braamfontein, Johannesburg CBD, Sandton, Randburg, Fourways, Midrand.

Questions about new building plumbing installations

Do you quote from drawings?

Yes. Send the plumbing drawings or bill of quantities and we can price the installation.

Do you work on high-rise buildings?

Yes. High-rise and multi-storey buildings are a core part of our work, including drainage stacks and booster pumps.
